## NEAR Discord draft

“We are looking for three people who have tried to gift Bitcoin to someone without a wallet or address, or who have used a physical bearer Bitcoin item. The first three will receive a small test Cachet note and should try the claim flow, then report what was confusing or useful. Reply here with the job you tried to do and the channel where you described it; please do not post seed phrases or private keys.”

## gov.near.org draft

“Cachet is validating a narrow user problem for the NFT track: handing over a small Bitcoin amount without requiring the recipient to start with a wallet or address, while making the note's ownership and claim state visible. We found three independent HN posts describing that job or its transfer mechanics, but we need live testers rather than more opinions. If you have gifted Bitcoin, used an Opendime or SATSCARD, or tried a paper Bitcoin gift, reply with the old workflow and volunteer to test one small public note.”
