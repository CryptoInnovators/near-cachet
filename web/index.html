How to check it

Each panel names its source and NEAR block below the result. Account and transaction ids open on nearblocks.io. Bitcoin addresses and txids open on mempool.space.

Cachet holds no admin key. The contract exposes no method that reaches the signer except an owner unseal or fee resign on an attested, unspent note.
