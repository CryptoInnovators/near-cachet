Gift a note
by link

The owner makes a link. Whoever opens it signs for their own NEAR account, and the contract moves the note to that account once.

Keys are made and used in this browser tab. The link secret sits after the # in the address, which browsers do not send to any server. Nothing is stored.

Make a claim link

What you are seeing: a fresh ed25519 key pair, the owner call that registers its public key on the note, and the link that carries the secret.

Claim a note

What you are seeing: the note this link points at, read from the contract, and the signature that binds the claim to the account you name.

Open a claim link to fill this in. A link looks like claim.html#v=1&contract=...&token=...&key=...

Reads

Fastnear mainnet RPC: block for the lock height and call_function for note.

How the contract checks it

claim verifies an ed25519 signature over cachet-claim:<contract>:<token>:<new owner> against the public key registered by create_claim. A signature for one account does not move the note to another.
